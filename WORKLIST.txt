- Update(): HP/MP regen timing, light decay, active effect expiry, disturbance checks.
- Movement & collision: Move(), HandleCollision(), CheckDisturbance() (doors/stairs/rubble set disturbed flag).
- Combat: Attack(), Damage(), Hit(), TakeDamage(), OnKillMonster() and effect handlers.
- Targeting: SetTarget/GetTarget and SetRangedHitPosition for ranged targeting.
- Light & intrinsics: LightSource(), UpdateLight(), DisplayStats() prints intrinsics.
- Wizard: SetWizard()/IsWizard() — debug/visibility toggles.
