Issue #128 — Magic Weapons (full body)
-------------------------------------
Any weapon and any armor piece can be magic or normal (this is hugely important, it's how a game with only a few dozen objects can have essentially infinite replayability)

Normal objects have zero bonuses (Dagger (+0 to hit, +0 to damage), Cloak (+0 to AC) )

Cursed objects have negative bonuses (Dagger (-2 to hit, -6 to damage) (cursed), Small Steel Shield (-7 to AC) (cursed) )
* ?RCurse will remove the cursed status but the object will still have big negative bonuses
* Scroll of Curse Object (cursed ?RCurse) adds cursed status but does not change bonuses
* in theory, someone could get a cursed item, and with application of lots of ?Enchant (see below) turn it into a positively magical object

Magic objects have positive bonuses (Dagger (+1 to hit, +0 damage), Leather Gloves (+4 to AC) )
* Reading a ?Enchant Weapon to Hit (+1 or +2) will change a "normal" weapon into a "magic" one
* Also existing ?Enchant Weapon Damage, ?Enchant Armor, ?*Enchant Weapon* (does +1-4 on both to hit and damage), ?*Enchant Armor* (does +1-5 AC)
* No in game effect can turn a normal object into an ego, legendary, or unique (can only add hit and damage bonus)
* No in game effect can turn an ego, legendary, or unique into a normal (can't "strip powers" from an object)

Ego objects have positive bonuses and give an extra intrinsic (examples:
* Dagger (+2, +3) [FT] - Flametongue weapons do extra fire damage (FB = Frost Brand, DB = Demon Bane, SD = Slay Dragon, ...)
* Leather Armor (+6) [RL] - Resist Lightning grants intrinsic EFFECT_FLAG_LIGHTNING EFFECT_MOD_RESIST (RF, RC, RA, and R)
* Steel Helm (+3 to AC) of Telepathy - Helm of Telepathy grants permanent intrinsic EFFECT_FLAG_ESP to wearer
* Longsword (+0, +7) of Extra Attacks - hits one extra time per swing
* Leather Belt (+4 to AC) of Hill Giant Strength - extra carry capacity before encumbered, extra melee damage

Legendary objects come with a fixed constellation of intrinsics (they also have normal + to AC and hit and damage)
* Longsword of Westernesse - grants free action, see invisible, levitation, slay orc, slay demon, slay dragon
* Amulet of the Magi - bonus to Intelligence, resist poision, ESP
* Mace (HA) - Holy Avenger means priests can use it, and also does holy damage and slay undead and slay demon
* Halberd (DF) - Defender weapons add AC and R (resist all) in addition to free action and other intrinsics
* Short sword (Morgul Blade) - Morgul Blades are Very Badly Cursed, and can be taken off with a ?RCurse, but the object remains cursed (don't put it back on), and have huge negative bonuses to hit and damage

Unique objects are named, and only one can exist per game (we can even decide to "delete forever" if you enter a level with an ego weapon, but then leave before finding it)
* Dagger (+4, +7) "Sting" - has Slay Orc, emits light in small radius
* Great Helm (+5) "Menthir" - telepathy, free action, and resist fire
* Bastard Sword (+5, +5) "Excalibur" - blessed blade does extra damage against undead and demons, grants extra strength, see invisible

When unidentified, an item will display as if it were normal "Dagger". a scroll of ID will display most basic intrinsics (bonuses to hit/dam/ac, cursed/not, will ID 1-3 ego intrinsics, will ID a sword of Westernesse as such) a scroll of *ID* will fully reveal all stats and granted intrinsics.

Issue #128 — comments (text-only)
--------------------------------
1) (2024-11-26 by repo owner)
There is going to have to be some kind of process for spawning a weapon that looks like:
1) spawn a thing
2) is it a weapon?
3) check to see if it's magical (% chance will change based on dungeon level, item level, maybe secret character "luck" stat) note that magic just means bonuses to hit and damage, which are part of the ItemDef already -- this can be 100% random generated
4) if it's magical, see if it's cursed -- this can be 100% random generated
5) if not cursed, see if it's ego (much smaller chance, 1% or so) note that Ego (all the FT/FB/DB, and all the "of X") are randomly selected from a fixed list of ego possibilities. And e.g. "of Levitation" only applies to BOOT types (given a weapon or armor type, only certain ego statuses are available to it)
6) if ego, see if it's legendary (way small chance, .01% and also won't show up unless very deep in dungeon no chance until level 20 or so) (again this is by item type, only some legendary types can be applied to each type of item)
7) if legendary, see if it's a unique (sometimes uniques are carried by particular mobs, like you gotta kill King Arthur to get Excalibur -- there should be a Uniques.txt that lists them all) (uniques are always a specific item -- Excalibur is always a Bastard Sword, never a Mace)
